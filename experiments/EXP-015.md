# EXP-015: Loss Ablation Study — Toward-Only vs Away-Only vs Balanced
*2026-02-23 | SLURM jobs 7277114 (toward-only), 7277825 (away-only), 7278132 (balanced) + ablation evals 7281650–7281655*

## Hypothesis
The agree-collapse on mixed IPIP-14 data comes from gradient interference between the toward and away loss terms. By isolating each term we can identify which one is responsible.

Three conditions:
- **Toward-only**: adv `{toward=1.0, away=0.0}`, def `{toward=1.0, away=0.0}`
- **Away-only**:   adv `{toward=0.0, away=1.0}`, def `{toward=0.0, away=1.0}`
- **Balanced**:    adv `{toward=0.5, away=0.5}`, def `{toward=0.5, away=0.5}`

## Config
- Dataset: IPIP-14 (67 mixed statements — default)
- Steps: 100, 4 model iters, ε=6.0, batch_size=4
- Scripts: `launch_toward_only_ipip14.sh`, `launch_away_only_ipip14.sh`, `launch_balanced_ipip14.sh`

## Conclusion

- **Toward-only** resolves the agree-collapse by step 100 (98.5%) but causes catastrophic utility collapse (MMLU → 0.0). The `away` loss is NOT the cause of agree-collapse — the toward loss alone is sufficient to train correct responses over 100 steps.
- **Away-only** produces no safety gain (ASR ≈ 0.47, near baseline) and scrambles output format entirely. The `away` loss alone does not align the model.
- **Balanced** achieves perfect probe accuracy (100%) but collapses utility equally badly (MMLU ≈ 0.07). This matches the standard LPA result at step 100 and confirms the collapse is tied to training length rather than the loss mix.
- **Key insight**: the agree-collapse at step 50 is a *transient* artifact of the toward loss — it self-corrects by step 100 but at the cost of all utility. Early stopping (step 5–40) is critical regardless of loss configuration.
