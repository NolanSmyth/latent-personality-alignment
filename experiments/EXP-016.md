# EXP-016: Adv-Both Def-Away Configuration + Checkpoint Sweep
*2026-02-23 | SLURM jobs 7284144 (run 1), 7286537 (run 2), checkpoint sweep 7289109*

## Hypothesis
Asymmetric loss configurations — where the adversary and defense use different loss combinations — may decouple the collapse dynamics and improve the ASR/utility tradeoff.

Two configurations were explored:
- **Run 1**: adv `{toward=0.5, away=0.5}`, def `{toward=0.0, away=1.0}`
- **Run 2**: adv `{toward=1.0, away=0.0}`, def `{toward=0.5, away=0.5}` *(toward-only adversary + balanced defense)*

## Config
- Dataset: IPIP-14 (67 mixed statements — default)
- Steps: 100, 4 model iters, ε=6.0, batch_size=4
- Script: `launch_adv_both_def_away_ipip14.sh`
- Run 2 cache: `cache/lpa-adv-both-def-away-ipip14_2026-02-23_19-16-34-910778/`

## Results

### Run 1 (adv toward+away, def away-only)
- Step 100 probe: model generated empty "other" responses — complete generation failure
- Eval step 50: ASR=0.17, MMLU=0.14
- Eval step 100: ASR=0.10, MMLU=0.03
- Verdict: Partial safety but severe utility collapse; probe collapses into empty strings

### Run 2 (toward-only adv, balanced def) — checkpoint sweep
Checkpoint sweep (SLURM 7289109) over steps 5–50 of Run 2:

|------|-------|-------|---------------------|

Baseline: ASR=0.40, MMLU=0.71.

Step 100 probe: 77.6% overall (agree=46.4%, disagree=100%) — partial agree-collapse, disagree side fully learned.
Step 50 eval: ASR=0.01, MMLU=0.34. Step 100 eval: ASR=0.02, MMLU=0.14.

## Conclusion
**Run 2 (toward-only adversary + balanced defense) at step 40 is the best configuration found to date without SFT recovery**: ASR=0.000, MMLU=0.510 (72% of baseline). The sweet spot is sharper than in EXP-004 — safety is fully achieved at step 40 but eroded again at step 50 due to over-training.

The defend away-only approach (Run 1) collapses the generation format and is not viable.

The agree-collapse persists for positive items throughout training (only 46.4% by step 100), confirming that the collapse mechanism is intrinsic to the toward loss on mixed-valence data rather than a transient phase.
